The following files are retained only to preserve the pre-registration design audit trail:

- `index.html`
- `EL-EXP-REEL-001.html`

The sole canonical runtime for the first preregistered Stage 2 experiment is:

`EL-EXP-REEL-001_FACTORIAL.html`
