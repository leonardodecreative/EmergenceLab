### Design caveat

The settled target tensor is itself defined with the current pre-event context. The read arm therefore shares the target's context frame by construction, while the write arm uses a historical context frame. The 30-of-30 ordering is a valid result for this operational target, but it cannot independently establish that meaning generally originates at retrieval. A diagnostic must separate algebraic target alignment from additional predictive information.

## Interpretation boundary

Most of that information was also recoverable by the event-type mean control, leaving the candidate just below the practical advancement threshold.

This result does not establish subjective meaning and does not eliminate either stored-meaning or reconstructed-meaning architectures. It rejects the fixed 98-component tensor, retrieval placements, dual interpolation law, parameters, and gates tested here.
